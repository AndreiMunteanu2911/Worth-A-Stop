Privacy

This school project stores account details and the reviews, collections and social content you submit. Public profile information and published content are visible to other visitors; email addresses are private to your account.

Place searches and selected locations are sent from our server to Google Places. If you request nearby places, your browser asks for location permission first. Map display connects your browser directly to Google Maps, which receives network information such as your IP address and the map area. Session cookies support login and request protection.

We retain Google place IDs to associate community content with places. Google place metadata is requested when needed and is not permanently stored by this application. Existing OpenStreetMap snapshots may remain for legacy locations.

Google's handling of data is explained in the Google Privacy Policy.
